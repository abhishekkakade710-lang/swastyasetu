# 🏥 SwasthyaSetu – Rural Public Healthcare Access Platform

**Healthcare Within Your Reach**

SwasthyaSetu is a digital healthcare platform designed to improve access to public healthcare services in rural and underserved areas. It helps people find nearby healthcare centres, check doctor availability, book appointments, and access medicine information and government healthcare schemes.

## 🎯 Aim

To make healthcare services more accessible, convenient, and efficient for people living in rural and underserved communities through digital technology.

## ❗ Problem Statement

People in rural areas often face difficulties finding nearby healthcare facilities, checking doctor availability, accessing medicine information, and learning about government healthcare schemes. SwasthyaSetu aims to address these challenges through a centralized digital platform.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔍 **Healthcare Centre Search:** Find nearby healthcare centres.
- 👨‍⚕️ **Doctor Availability:** Check doctor schedules and availability.
- 📅 **Appointment Booking:** Book and manage appointments.
- 💊 **Medicine Information:** Access information about medicine availability.
- 🏛️ **Government Healthcare Schemes:** Explore relevant healthcare schemes.
- 🌐 **Multilingual Support:** English, Hindi, and Marathi.
- 👤 **Role-Based Dashboards:** Separate interfaces for patients, doctors, hospital staff, and administrators.
- 🚑 **Emergency Assistance:** Access emergency assistance information.
- 📊 **Admin Dashboard:** Manage healthcare information and view reports.
- ⭐ **Feedback System:** Submit feedback about healthcare services.

## 👥 User Roles

1. **Patient:** Search healthcare centres, check availability, and book appointments.
2. **Doctor:** View appointments and manage availability.
3. **Hospital Staff:** Manage appointments, medicines, and healthcare centre information.
4. **Administrator:** Manage users, monitor services, and review reports.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Improve access to public healthcare services.
- Reduce the time required to find suitable healthcare facilities.
- Help patients check doctor availability before visiting.
- Provide healthcare information in regional languages.
- Support better management of healthcare services.
